All fonts are bundled locally for offline desktop use.

Poppins (weights 400–900, Latin/Devanagari): https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/poppins
License: Poppins-OFL.txt. Poppins has no Cyrillic; Roboto is the fallback for Russian text.

Roboto (variable, Latin/Cyrillic): https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/roboto
License: Roboto-OFL.txt.

JetBrains Mono (variable, weights 100–800, Latin/Cyrillic): https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/jetbrainsmono
License: JetBrainsMono-OFL.txt. Used for timers and numeric indicators.

Relative URLs in fonts.css support packaged Electron and local UI tests.
